N°01 The Collective

Making waves where beauty & art collide. Only Models is a creator collective out of Ghana and South Africa, showcasing African beauty through fashion shows, content creation and brand partnerships.

We’re a house of creators who shoot together, travel together and show up for each other’s channels. That’s why the content feels like a group chat and not a casting call.

One brief can reach every corner of the collective: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and the creators’ own audiences.

What we do for brands
  • i.

    Content creation

    Try-on hauls, lookbooks, game shows and shorts, shot on location and in studio.

  • ii.

    Fashion shows

    Runway, swim weeks and live appearances with creators who know how to hold a room.

  • iii.

    Brand partnerships

    Your product worn, styled and talked about by faces an audience already trusts.
